Personal Finance Vocabulary

Klik kartu istilah untuk melihat definisi dan konteks perbankan:

Income Money received, especially on a regular basis, for work or through investments.
Expense The cost required for something; money spent on living costs and transactions.
Savings Money saved or preserved, typically through a bank account.
Budget An estimate of income and expenditure for a set period of time.
Debt An amount of money borrowed by one party from another, to be paid back later.

08

Empat Produk Perbankan Inti

Savings Account

For daily transactions and flexible liquid savings. Lower interest rate, high liquidity.

Time Deposit

Fixed-term investment account with higher interest rates. Penalty applies for early withdrawal.

Loan

Borrowed capital provided by the bank, repaid over time with agreed interest rates.

Debit / Credit Card

Payment tools linked directly to account funds (Debit) or extended credit lines (Credit).
